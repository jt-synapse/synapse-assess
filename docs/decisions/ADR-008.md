# ADR-008 — Consented reasoning transcription

**Status** Proposed for formal ratification. Provider and retention periods remain open.

**Context.** Spoken technical explanations can help a human reviewer interpret changes and test results. Audio and transcripts are sensitive.

**Decision.** Display the collection notice, obtain consent, and then request microphone access in the browser. Authenticate the stream, bound the buffer, link final segments to session and stage time, support auditable correction, and provide a written alternative.

**Alternatives.** Continuous recording before consent violates the collection requirement. A post-session recording cannot show interruptions as they happen.

**Rationale.** A visible, interruptible stream supports contextual evidence and an accessible alternative.

**Consequences.** Raw audio and transcript retention require approved policies and deletion controls. Rubrics may consider the technical explanation only.

**Validation.** Test denied permission, revocation, reconnect, duplicate final segments, corrections, deletion, and completion using written reasoning throughout.
