# ADR-006 — Append-only assessment evidence

**Status** Proposed for formal ratification. SEC-009 applies to every evidence producer.

**Context.** Browser actions, broker-observed commands, and transcripts arrive on different clocks and may be retried.

**Decision.** Assign an authoritative per-session sequence, ingestion time, and schema version on the server. Deduplicate retries by stable identity. Store immutable artifacts with integrity references. Represent corrections as new events referencing the original.

**Alternatives.** Mutable activity summaries lose the original observation. Client timestamps alone cannot resolve ingestion order.

**Rationale.** A reviewer needs a reproducible link from each judgment to the original action and any correction.

**Consequences.** Retention and authorized deletion must cover sensitive payloads, artifacts, replicas, and the audit of deletion under the approved policy. Access to reviewer evidence needs independent authorization.

**Validation.** Deliver duplicates and out-of-order events concurrently, replay after resource cleanup, check artifact integrity, and verify correction history and authorized deletion.
